Tile Flooring Installation

Modern architectural tile has evolved far beyond standard grid ceramics. We specialize in handling and setting ultra-wide gauged porcelain slabs (up to 60"×120"), rectified through-body Italian porcelain, hand-molded Moroccan Zellige, and geometric waterjet mosaics.

Using directional troweling and ultrasonic vibration bedding, we achieve 98%+ mortar contact—eliminating hollow spots, preventing corner fractures, and maximizing thermal conductivity over radiant floor heating systems.

Material Characteristics
  • • Impervious water absorption (≤ 0.05%)
  • • PEI Class IV & V abrasion immunity
  • • UV, frost, and chemical stain resistant
Primary Applications
  • • Open-plan living & culinary galleries
  • • Curbless spa showers & wellness suites
  • • Seamless indoor-to-outdoor lanais

Marble Flooring Installation

Natural marble is the ultimate expression of architectural permanence and organic artistry. Because no two marble slabs are identical, our master setters perform a complete CAD dry-lay prior to installation—orchestrating continuous bookmatched or diamond-matched veining across hallways, thresholds, and grand staircases.

We exclusively use low-alkali, non-staining white polymer-modified mortars to prevent moisture shadowing, followed by multi-stage planetary diamond honing and deep-penetrating fluoropolymer sealing.

Material Characteristics
  • • Translucent crystalline calcite depth
  • • Bookmatched & vein-matched continuity
  • • Lifetime refinishing & re-polishing capability
Primary Applications
  • • Double-height estate foyers &rotundas
  • • Formal dining salons & primary baths
  • • Custom brass-inlaid border medallions

Granite Flooring Installation

Forged under extreme geological pressure, natural granite and crystalline quartzite offer the highest structural density and scratch resistance of any natural dimension stone. Pietra Vera installs calibrated 20mm and 30mm granite flooring for clients who demand uncompromising durability paired with understated luxury.

Available in mirror-polished, satin-honed, leathered cashmere, and thermal-flamed anti-slip finishes, our granite floors transition effortlessly from high-traffic commercial concourses to gourmet kitchens and exterior pool decks.

Material Characteristics
  • • Mohs 6.5 – 7.0 diamond-grade hardness
  • • Complete immunity to culinary acids & heat
  • • 29,000+ PSI compressive load rating
Primary Applications
  • • Corporate & boutique hotel lobbies
  • • Heavy-use chef kitchens & wine cellars
  • • Freeze-thaw exterior colonnades & plazas

Turnkey Execution

Our 5-Step Precision Installation Workflow

From initial laser topography to final diamond buffing, every phase is executed by our certified in-house artisans.

01

Site Laser Survey & Sample Curation

We measure exact room geometry, test concrete moisture vapor emission rates, and evaluate stone slabs under your room’s natural lighting.

02

Dust-Free Demo & Self-Leveling

HEPA-contained removal of old flooring followed by diamond scarifying and pouring fiber-reinforced self-leveling underlayment to FF50 flatness.

03

Waterproofing & Crack Isolation

Application of Schluter decoupling membranes, electric/hydronic radiant heat cables, and acoustic sound-control barriers.

04

Laser-Guided Setting & Lippage Calibration

Slabs and tiles are back-buttered for 98% mortar coverage and locked with mechanical leveling systems for sub-millimeter planar accuracy.

05

Epoxy Grouting, Honing & 15-Year Warranty Hand-Off

Color-matched stain-proof epoxy grouting, planetary diamond finishing, penetrating sealer application, and delivery of your maintenance care kit.
