The Pietra Vera Standard

Why Discerning Architects & Estate Owners Entrust Us

A luxury stone floor is only as enduring as the subfloor engineering beneath it. Unlike general contractors, our in-house master setters execute every stage without subcontractors.

ANSI A108.02 Zero-Lippage Guarantee

Every slab joint is mechanically calibrated and diamond-dressed in situ so light reflects across your room as a single, unbroken surface.

Discover Our Craftsmanship Standards
01

Laser Subfloor Remediation

Rotary laser mapping identifies structural dips within 0.5mm. We pour fiber-reinforced self-leveling underlayments and epoxy moisture barriers prior to setting.

02

Direct Quarry Block Selection

Through direct partnerships in Carrara, Novelda, and Espírito Santo, we source sequential slab bundles from the same quarry block for seamless vein flow.

03

Crack-Isolation & Acoustic Membranes

Every installation includes uncoupling and sound-attenuation membranes (IIC 72 rated) that prevent structural slab movement from transferring to your stone.

04

HEPA Dust-Free Execution

We protect occupied residences and fine millwork using negative-air containment, wet bridge saws, and stain-proof 100% solids epoxy grouts.
