Our Story & Provenance

Built on the Belief That a Floor Is the Fifth Elevation of Architecture

When master stone mason Lorenzo Bellini founded Pietra Vera in 1998, he recognized a critical flaw in modern luxury construction: extraordinary marble, granite, and porcelain slabs were frequently compromised by rushed subfloor preparation and uncalibrated setting.

Over the past 28 years, Pietra Vera has grown into an elite architectural flooring contractor trusted by premier residential architects, private estate owners, and luxury hospitality developers. We never subcontract our installations—every setter on our roster completes a rigorous four-year apprenticeship in substrate leveling, CAD bookmatching, and in-situ diamond finishing.

Engineering & Sourcing Standards

Uncompromising Quality Benchmarks & Quarry Curation

We exceed North American and European stone setting codes on every residential and commercial floor we touch.

Standard 01

TCNA & ANSI A108.02 Compliance

All substrates are prepared to meet or exceed Tile Council of North America (TCNA) EJ171 movement joint guidelines and ANSI A108 flatness tolerances before setting begins.

Verified by Laser Profilometer
Standard 02

Direct Quarry Block Inspection

We reject commercial-grade slabs with resin patches, structural fissures, or inconsistent background tones. Our buyers source First-Choice (Prima Scelta) blocks directly from Italy, Spain, and Brazil.

Mission & Core Values

Our Architectural Covenant With Every Client

Our mission is to transform architectural floors into enduring works of stone craftsmanship—delivered on schedule, within the agreed quotation, and with absolute respect for your property.

“True luxury in stone setting is measured twenty years after installation—when not a single joint has shifted and the surface still catches the morning light like glass.”
— Lorenzo Bellini, Founder & Master Stone Setter

1. Precision Without Compromise

Whether setting a 300 sq. ft. powder room mosaic or a 12,000 sq. ft. granite concourse, we hold every cut, mitre, and threshold to sub-millimeter tolerances.

2. Fixed-Price Transparency

Our itemized quotations detail slab waste factors, subfloor leveling compounds, membranes, and finishing—eliminating unexpected change orders.

3. White-Glove Site Stewardship

We protect pathways with Ram Board, isolate work zones with HEPA air scrubbers, and leave your residence immaculate at the close of every workday.

4. 15-Year Structural Bond Warranty

We stand behind every installation with a written 15-year warranty against bond failure, subfloor delamination, and structural joint cracking.
