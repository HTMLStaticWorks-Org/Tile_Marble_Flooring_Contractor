Engineering Methodology

Our 5-Stage Architectural Installation Protocol

Every commission follows a documented quality-control sequence verified by our project superintendent and backed by a 15-year structural warranty.

Stage 01

Laser Topography & Moisture Diagnostics

Calcium chloride moisture vapor emission testing (ASTM F1869) and dual-grade laser mapping to correct subfloor deflection before work begins.

Stage 02

CAD Vein Mapping & Dry-Lay Approval

We dry-lay natural stone slabs in our facility and generate a numbered placement blueprint so doorways, islands, and sightlines align symmetrically.

Stage 03

Self-Leveling & Decoupling Membrane

Installation of Schluter Ditra-Heat or liquid-applied crack-isolation membranes to isolate the stone from structural concrete shrinkage or joist movement.

Stage 04

Full-Contact Mortar Bedding & Mechanical Tuning

Using ultrasonic vibration plates and precision leveling clips, we eliminate hollow voids beneath slabs and achieve a zero-lippage surface across thousands of square feet.

Stage 05

Epoxy Joint Grouting, Diamond Honing & Fluoropolymer Sealing

Stain-proof epoxy or micro-fine mineral grouts are applied, followed by planetary diamond buffing and hospital-grade impregnating sealers for effortless daily maintenance.
